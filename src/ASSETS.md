# WinterDesk 1.7 assets

Three embedded resources: active DrawnPack, DecorSheet, PenguinIcon. No greeting groups, old executables, source bundles or development checks are embedded.

New worker-keys.png: sixteen close-spaced full-body shoveling drawings with both orange feet grounded, no greeting and no detached leg. New snow-roll-keys.png: sixteen consecutive low forward-bent rolling poses. Both are aligned by their actual feet, rather than the changing width of the shovel/arms. snow-balls-keys.png contains eight real painted opaque snow spheres in the same early-Disney-inspired visual language. These replace the prior procedural gradient/dot balls for both hand and builder interactions.

Interpolation and packing helpers: animation_frames.py and assets17.py. They use Python, Pillow, NumPy, SciPy and OpenCV DIS. The production EXE needs only the already packed resources. The whole-Santa team group from 1.6 is preserved as integrated drawings, with no runtime animal assembly.

Scarf recoloring uses locked premultiplied buffers and caches tinted source frames before scaling. Orange carrot/gold hues and dark outlines are excluded from the cloth tint. Character artwork does not use grafted greeting limbs or sheared standing snowman cutouts.
